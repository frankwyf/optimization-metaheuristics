## Interpretation

- PSO/DE results are unchanged for these seeds and settings. Their implementation and RNG behavior were preserved in this upgrade.
- GA/SA may change because their returned incumbents were corrected. This is evidence of corrected result semantics, not a broad optimization-quality claim.
- Before/after convergence diagnostics (including nonmonotone histories) and all per-run results are retained in the accompanying JSON. GA/SA histories now include initialization; compare values and evaluation counts rather than matching history indices.
- No seeds or infeasible runs were excluded. No significance test or global-optimality claim is made.
